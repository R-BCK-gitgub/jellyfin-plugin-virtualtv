How this preview is structured

Channels are exposed through Jellyfin Live TV and Guide. The schedule is persisted separately from personal viewing state, while Jellyfin remains responsible for playback, audio, subtitles and player controls.
✓
30-day schedule horizon
Materialized schedules are retained and extended without rebuilding past blocks.
✓
7-day schedule history
The weekly Extend task removes only history older than seven days.
✓
Native Jellyfin Scheduled Tasks
Extend runs Monday at 03:00 by default; Reconcile runs daily at 05:00. Both remain editable in Jellyfin Scheduled Tasks.

Channel modes

Traditional
Sequential and Random / Shuffle Cycle use a stable materialized grid and ignore personal Resume for Live entry.
Watched-dependent
Next Unwatched and Random Unwatched are personal admin-only channels in v1 and resolve against Jellyfin viewing state.

Functional validation

This preview implements the final configuration model, persistent scheduling, native Live TV/Guide exposure and the v1.0.12 playback path. Items below separate implemented behavior from stock-client limitations that still require architectural proof.

Non-negotiable v1 invariants represented in this preview

✓
Traditional channels use a wall-clock schedule and never use personal Resume to decide Live position.
✓
Schedules are persisted; past/current blocks are protected during Reconcile and Generate New Schedule.
✓
Random means Shuffle Cycle for materialized scheduling, including no immediate cycle-boundary repeat where possible.
✓
Smart scheduling uses Sunday-to-Saturday boundaries, rotation periods and full-week future extension.
✓
Off Air, Content Not Available and Schedule Not Available are distinct states.
